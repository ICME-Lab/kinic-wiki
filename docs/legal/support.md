# Support

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

Kinic provides support for Kinic Wiki and the Kinic Wiki ChatGPT app.

For questions, bug reports, or access problems, contact us at [https://x.com/kinic_app](https://x.com/kinic_app).

## 1. Services we support

This support page covers:

- Kinic Wiki.
- The Kinic Wiki ChatGPT app.

## 2. Contact support

Contact us through the Kinic account at [https://x.com/kinic_app](https://x.com/kinic_app) for:

- Questions about using a supported service.
- Bug reports or unexpected behavior.
- Problems accessing Kinic Wiki or the Kinic Wiki ChatGPT app.

## 3. What to include

To help us investigate, include:

- Your device, operating system, browser, ChatGPT client, or other relevant environment details.
- The action you were trying to perform.
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
- Clear steps that reproduce the problem, when possible.

Screenshots and exact error messages can be helpful, but remove private or sensitive information before sharing them.

## 4. Protect your secrets

Do not send passwords, private keys, seed phrases, API keys, or authentication tokens in a support report.

We do not need these secrets to investigate a question or problem. If you believe a secret has been exposed, revoke or rotate it through the service that issued it.

## 5. Response times

We aim to provide an initial response within 3 business days. Complex investigations may take longer, but we will provide an update when possible.

For information about how Kinic processes and protects information, read the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).
